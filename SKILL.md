# Bookkeeping Starter Kit — Skill

General guidance only — not tax, legal, or accounting advice. Recommend a qualified professional for the founder's specific situation.

## Files in this kit

- `chart-of-accounts.csv` — starter chart (~43 accounts) to import into QuickBooks, Xero, Wave, Zoho Books, or a spreadsheet.
- `monthly-routine.md` — weekly 15-minute habit, monthly close checklist, quarterly review.
- `records-and-red-flags.md` — what to keep, how long, how to store it, and 8 red flags.

## Rule zero: separate business account — nothing else works without it

Never mix personal and business money. Commingling is a classic reason courts and auditors treat owner and business as the same thing; a dedicated account makes categorising minutes-fast, taxes defensible, and processors / banks satisfied. Practical setup from the repo: one business checking account, one business savings account for tax reserves, one business-only credit card, processors paying out only to business checking.

Owner money is recorded precisely: money in = **Owner Contributions (3000)**, money out = **Owner Draws (3010)**. Draws are not salary and not a business expense.

## Setting up

1. Import `chart-of-accounts.csv`; delete accounts never used, rename to match the business, keep categories broad — do not create an account per vendor.
2. Connect / record consistently: every transaction gets one account from the chart, no free-text categories. Uncategorized (`6990 Uncategorized / Ask My Accountant`) must be zero at every close.
3. Record processor activity gross: gross sales as income, processing fees as an expense, then reconcile payouts — never record the net payout as revenue.

## Weekly 15-minute habit

Check bank / card feeds for anything unfamiliar or personal-by-mistake; categorise new transactions while memory is fresh; save the week's receipts; chase overdue invoices; move the tax set-aside to business savings when a payout lands.

## Monthly close — a closed month means

Every transaction entered and categorised; every account (checking, savings, cards, processors) reconciled to its statement; receipts matched; P&L and Balance Sheet reviewed and sensible; tax money set aside. Follow `monthly-routine.md` in order: gather → reconcile → categorise and clean up (split mixed transactions, split loan payments principal vs interest) → match receipts (note business purpose / attendees for meals and travel) → invoicing and receivables → review P&L vs prior month → set aside tax money at a percentage agreed with a tax professional → file the month's documents in a consistently named folder and lock the period. If every month is closed, year-end is just the twelfth close.

Quarterly: review P&L vs budget / prior quarter and top expense categories, review the estimated-tax position with a professional, and check state obligations (annual report / renewal dates, sales tax frequency, notices).

## Records

Keep income records (invoices, processor and marketplace reports, contracts), expense records (receipts showing date, vendor, amount, what was bought), and business / tax records (formation documents, EIN letter CP-575 / 147C, operating agreement, filed returns and workpapers, statements, loan and lease agreements, W-9 / W-8 records where applicable). Retention in the repo: tax returns, supporting records, and statements at least 7 years; formation / ownership / property / loan documents for as long as relevant plus at least 7 years after the related final return / closure; check state rules. Store digitally in one consistently named system, capture receipts the same day, back up in two places, control access, and export out of the accounting app monthly — do not leave the only copy inside it. Collect contractor W-9 / W-8 forms before the first payment, not in January.

## Red flags — surface these early

Commingling; missing receipts (a bank line proves money moved, not what it was for); personal expenses left coded as business (recode to Owner Draws promptly); uncategorized buildup; ignored state filings; no tax reserve; net-payout revenue recording; contractor paperwork chased at year-end. Fixes for each are in `records-and-red-flags.md`.

## For non-resident founders

A US LLC owned by a non-resident still files US federal returns every year — even at $0 income. Clean, closed books make Form 1120 / 5472, Form 1065, or Schedule C / 1040-NR preparation simpler and cheaper. Hand the preparer a closed set of books, not a bank feed and a hope. Note the scale of getting Form 5472 wrong: a late or incomplete Form 5472 penalty starts at $25,000 — which is why complete records matter.
